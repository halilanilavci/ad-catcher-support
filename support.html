Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I turn Ad Catcher on, on iPhone or iPad?
A: Open Ad Catcher and tap Turn On in Safari. Or, in Safari, tap the page menu button next to the address bar, then Manage Extensions, and turn on Ad Catcher Ads and Ad Catcher Trackers. You can also use Settings › Apps › Safari › Extensions.

Q: And on Mac?
A: Open Ad Catcher and click Turn On in Safari: Safari's Extensions settings open. Tick Ad Catcher Ads and Ad Catcher Trackers. You can also find them in Safari › Settings › Extensions.

Q: A site doesn't work properly. What can I do?
A: Open Ad Catcher, choose Allowed Sites, add the site's address and reload the page in Safari. Nothing is blocked on allowed sites. Each device keeps its own list.

Q: Does it block ads in other apps or browsers?
A: No. Safari's content blockers work in Safari and in pages that apps open with Safari. Ads shown inside other apps, and other browsers, are not covered.

Q: Can Ad Catcher see the sites I visit?
A: No. Safari applies Ad Catcher's rules by itself and never shows the app your browsing. Ad Catcher does not connect to the internet.

Q: Where do the rules come from?
A: From EasyList and EasyPrivacy (easylist.to) by The EasyList authors, converted for Safari, under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 license. They are updated with each app update.
